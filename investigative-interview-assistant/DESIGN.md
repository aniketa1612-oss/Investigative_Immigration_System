# Design System

## Theme

A quiet evidence-room workspace in clear daylight: restrained, precise, and free of dramatic policing imagery.

## Color

- Background: `oklch(1 0 0)`
- Surface: `oklch(0.965 0.008 180)`
- Ink: `oklch(0.22 0.025 190)`
- Muted: `oklch(0.46 0.025 190)`
- Primary: `oklch(0.50 0.10 180)`
- Accent: `oklch(0.68 0.15 55)`
- Danger: `oklch(0.52 0.18 25)`

## Typography

Use Inter with a system sans-serif fallback. Keep a compact product scale with tabular numerals for timing and confidence values.

## Components

Controls use one consistent 10px radius, high-contrast focus rings, explicit labels, and text plus colour for states. Panels rely on spacing and surface shifts rather than wide shadows.

## Layout

A persistent status header, narrow control rail, and flexible live workspace. Collapse to one column below tablet width; keep capture controls sticky on small screens.

## Motion

Use 160–220ms transitions for state feedback only. Disable non-essential transitions when reduced motion is requested.
