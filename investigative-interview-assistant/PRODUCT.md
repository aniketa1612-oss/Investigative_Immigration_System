# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Authorized immigration officers conducting structured interviews at airports, border-entry points, visa offices, or consulates. They need a calm, legible workspace that supports relevant questioning without suggesting that an automated system can determine deception, admissibility, or visa eligibility.

## Product Purpose

Provide consent-based capture, multilingual transcription, behavioural observations, neutral immigration follow-up suggestions, timelines, and structured reports. Success means officers can review the applicant or traveller's recorded account, cautious interpretations, and procedural follow-ups while retaining all official judgment.

## Brand Personality

Calm, evidence-led, accountable.

## Anti-references

Not a lie-detector interface, surveillance theatre, a futuristic command centre, or a gamified risk score. Avoid guilt labels, alarming red/green judgments, biometric certainty, and hidden recording states.

## Design Principles

- Make capture and consent status impossible to miss.
- Separate observed, interpreted, and recommended information.
- Preserve the investigator's attention during live interviews.
- State uncertainty and Demo Mode at the point of use.
- Support Indian and international languages without treating one accent, dialect, or region as the default.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, non-colour state cues, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and language labels written in both English and native scripts where practical.
